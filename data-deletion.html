Pickle Finder – Data Deletion Request

This page explains how to request deletion of information associated with the Pickle Finder application.

1. Request deletion by email

Pickle Finder does not currently provide in-app account creation or an account deletion button. To request deletion of information associated with an online Paddle Q session created in an earlier version, email us:

Email: picklefinder2026@gmail.com
Subject: Pickle Finder Data Deletion Request

Include enough information to identify the relevant records:

  • Session name or venue.
  • Approximate session date.
  • Player or organizer display name, if applicable.
  • Session identifier, if available.

Do not email passwords, organizer secrets, player credentials, or other sensitive authentication information.

2. What information can be deleted?

Depending on the records involved, requests may cover information associated with online Paddle Q sessions created in earlier versions, including:

  • Session and venue details.
  • Schedules and session information.
  • Player display names and participant lists.
  • Game information, scores, and results.
  • Related access records.

Online Share Session and Join Session features are unavailable in the current release. Local-only Paddle Q sessions are stored on your device and are not automatically uploaded to the online service.

We review deletion requests subject to applicable requirements and technical limitations. Information that must be retained for legal obligations or security purposes may not be immediately deletable.

3. Remove information stored on your device

Pickle Finder stores certain information locally, such as favorite venues, planned schedules, preferences, and local-only Paddle Q sessions.

You can remove locally stored information by:

  • Using available removal controls within the app.
  • Clearing Pickle Finder's app storage through your device settings.
  • Uninstalling the application.

The exact steps depend on your device and operating system.

Clearing local app storage does not delete information previously submitted to an online service. For online session information from earlier versions, email us using the address above.

4. Processing of requests

We will review each request and may contact you if additional information is needed to identify the relevant records.

Processing time depends on the information involved, applicable requirements, and technical limitations.

We do not promise an automatic deletion schedule or a fixed processing deadline. Pickle Finder does not currently claim that online session records are automatically deleted after a set number of days.
