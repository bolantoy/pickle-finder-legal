1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Pickle Finder collects, stores, uses, and protects information when you use the application.

Pickle Finder helps users discover pickleball venues, check venue information and availability, plan playing sessions, and organize games through Paddle Q.

The application is operated by Russel Dimalig, using the public developer name RRD. By using Pickle Finder, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Pickle Finder stores certain information locally on your device to provide its features. This may include:

  • Favorite venues.
  • Planned playing sessions and schedules.
  • Theme and display preferences.
  • Remembered Paddle Q session information, such as display name, venue or session name, date, and participant role.
  • Local-only Paddle Q sessions, including player names and game information.

This information is stored using the application's local storage facilities. Local-only Paddle Q sessions are controlled by you on your device and are not automatically uploaded to the online Paddle Q service.

Organizer and player credentials used by Paddle Q are stored separately using the device's secure storage facility. They are not included in the remembered-session metadata described above.

3. Information Processed Through Online Paddle Q

If you use online Paddle Q features, information may be transmitted to and stored by the application's backend service.

Depending on how you use the feature, this information may include:

  • Session and venue information.
  • Session dates and times.
  • Court counts and reservations.
  • Session status.
  • Player display names.
  • Game participants.
  • Game scores and results.
  • Credential hashes used to support organizer and player access.

Online Paddle Q information is used to create, manage, and display sessions, coordinate participants, and record game results.

Do not use sensitive personal information as a player display name or include it in session or game information.

4. Device Location

Pickle Finder does not request or read your device's current GPS location.

The application uses venue coordinates obtained from venue information or configured venue data to display locations and open directions in external mapping applications such as Google Maps.

When you choose to open an external mapping application, that application operates under its own privacy policy and terms. Pickle Finder does not control how the external mapping provider processes information.

5. Internet Services and Third Parties

Pickle Finder uses external services to provide its functionality. These may include:

  • Supabase: Database services used by the backend for online Paddle Q information.
  • Render: Backend application hosting.
  • Expo services: Application infrastructure and update delivery.
  • Google Maps: External map and directions functionality when opened by the user.
  • External venue websites and booking services: Venue information, availability, and booking links.

When you use features that rely on these services, relevant information may be transmitted to the service involved.

External services have their own privacy policies, security practices, and terms. We do not control the independent privacy practices of third-party services.

6. Analytics and Advertising

The application source reviewed for this policy did not identify dedicated analytics or advertising SDK integrations.

This statement describes the reviewed application implementation and does not guarantee that third-party infrastructure providers collect no operational or technical information. Their own services may process technical information as described in their respective policies.

7. Data Retention

Information stored locally on your device remains under your control. Pickle Finder does not currently apply the planned 30-day server-side cleanup policy to local-only sessions.

For online Paddle Q sessions, active sessions are not automatically deleted.

The intended retention policy is to delete completed and cancelled online sessions, together with their associated player and game records, after 30 days. This automatic cleanup is planned but is not yet confirmed as deployed. Until the migration and scheduled cleanup service are implemented and verified, we cannot promise that this 30-day schedule is operational.

You may request deletion of online Paddle Q information at any time by contacting us using the email address below. We will review and process requests subject to applicable requirements and technical limitations.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures intended to protect information handled by the application.

These include secure local storage for organizer and player credentials and server-side handling of online Paddle Q data. Credential values stored in the online database are represented using hashes rather than being stored as ordinary readable credentials.

However, no application, storage system, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should keep your device secure and avoid sharing organizer or player credentials with other people.

9. Children's Privacy and Intended Audience

Pickle Finder is intended for a general audience, including adults and older teenagers.

The application is not specifically designed to collect personal information from young children. Users should not submit sensitive personal information through display names, session details, or other application features.

If you believe a child has submitted personal information through the application, please contact us so we can review the matter.

10. Your Choices and Data Requests

You can choose whether to use online Paddle Q features. You can also choose whether to save local favorites, schedules, and local-only sessions.

When you leave Pickle Finder and use an external service, that service's own terms and privacy policy apply. We are not responsible for the content, availability, or privacy practices of external websites and services.
